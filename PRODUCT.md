# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Windows 11 desktop interface with a clear, light, matte visual language.

## Users

Students who want to manage their school timetable, agenda, and documents on their Windows computer.

## Product Purpose

SchoolDesk brings a student's timetable, school tasks, quick notes, and Word documents into one locally available application. Success means being able to see what is next and organize school work without needing an account or cloud service.

## Positioning

A native, offline-first school workspace that keeps its data on the student's own computer.

## Operating Context

Used on a Windows desktop or laptop during the school day and while doing homework. The application opens to a dashboard and provides integrated pages for the agenda, DOCX documents, file explorer, quick notes, and settings.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The timetable supports A/B weeks and is configured locally per user.
- The agenda stores dated tasks and completion state.
- The document editor reads and writes common DOCX content; it is not a full Microsoft Word layout engine.
- User data is stored locally. The application does not require an account, network connection, or cloud service.
- Windows startup is optional and user-configurable.

## Brand Commitments

The application is named SchoolDesk and uses French for its interface. Its user-approved visual direction is light, matte, and native to Windows; Liquid Glass, decorative translucency, and blur are rejected.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the WPF application and its README. The bundled timetable template is empty so users can supply their own schedule.

## Product Principles

- Keep school information available locally.
- Make today's schedule and next task easy to understand.
- Keep core workflows in one application window.
- Do not invent timetable data for users.
